Geometric Abstraction

to strip art of all ties to the physical world, turning a painting into a "thing in itself" rather than a copy of external reality

aimed to turn the act of perception itself into the subject of the artwork

  • Artist: Josef Albers
  • Title: Homage to the Square
  • Date: 1969
  • Media: Oil on Masonite

A famous, decades-long series investigating how adjacent colors optically interact and alter one anothers appearance. Albers used the simple shape of the square to demonstrate that human perception of hue and placement is relative and constantly shifting

Plan: Use a box model with different colored content,padding, border increasing in size.

Neoplasticism

Artists sought pure abstraction by reducing art to its most essential elements: straight lines, right angles, primary colors and neutral tones

aimed to create a universal visual language that could bring order to the world following the chaos of World War I

  • Artist: Piet Mondrian
  • Title: Composition No. IV, with Red, Blue and Yellow
  • Date: 1929
  • Media: Oil painting

An abstract oil painting utilizing primary colors red,yellow and blue. Mondrian used only straight vertical and horizontal black lines to form a grid believing it expressed a spiritual balance and order common to all people.

Plan: Use box model border width for black grids and content as color
